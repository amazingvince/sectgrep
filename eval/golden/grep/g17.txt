cfr-title-99/I/A/2/2.12/99-2.12.md:37:(b) *Lighting and marking.* Each exit route shall be adequately lighted so that an employee with normal vision can see along the exit route. Each exit shall be clearly visible and marked by a sign reading "Exit" in plainly legible letters at least 6 inches (15 cm) high. Each doorway or passage along an exit access that could be mistaken for an exit shall be marked "Not an Exit" or with a sign identifying its actual use. Where the direction of travel to the exit is not immediately apparent, signs shall be posted along the exit access indicating the direction of travel.
