cfr-title-99/I/A/2/2.10/99-2.10.md:37:(a) *Number of exit routes.* At least two exit routes, as defined in [§ 2.2](CFR:99-2.2#exit-route), shall be available in a workplace to permit prompt evacuation of employees and other occupants during an emergency. The exit routes shall be located as far away as practicable from each other so that if one exit route is blocked by fire or smoke, employees can evacuate using the second. More than two exit routes shall be provided where the number of employees, the size of the building, its occupancy, or the arrangement of the workplace is such that all employees would not be able to evacuate safely through two. A single exit route is permitted where the number of employees, the size of the building, its occupancy, or the arrangement of the workplace is such that all employees would be able to evacuate safely through one.
