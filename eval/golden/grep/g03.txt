cfr-title-99/I/A/2/2.2/99-2.2.md:56:*Unprotected sides and edges* means any side or edge of a walking-working surface, except at entrances and other points of access, where there is no wall, guardrail system, or stair rail system to protect an employee from falling to a lower level.
cfr-title-99/I/A/2/2.8/99-2.8.md:36:(a) *Top rail height.* The top edge height of a top rail, or equivalent guardrail system member, shall be 42 inches (107 cm), plus or minus 3 inches (8 cm), above the walking-working surface. The top edge height may exceed 45 inches (114 cm), provided the guardrail system meets all other criteria of this section.
cfr-title-99/I/A/2/2.8/99-2.8.md:40:(c) *Strength.* A guardrail system shall be capable of withstanding, without failure, a force of at least 200 pounds (890 N) applied in a downward or outward direction within 2 inches (5 cm) of the top edge, at any point along the top rail. Midrails, screens, mesh, and intermediate members shall be capable of withstanding a force of at least 150 pounds (667 N).
cfr-title-99/I/A/2/2.8/99-2.8.md:44:(e) *Construction.* A guardrail system shall be smooth-surfaced to protect employees from injury and to prevent snagging of clothing. The ends of top rails and midrails shall not overhang the terminal posts, except where the overhang does not constitute a projection hazard. Steel or plastic banding shall not be used as a top rail or midrail.

city-amendments/AM-1.md:38:(a) *Top rail height.* The top edge height of a top rail, or equivalent guardrail system member, shall be 48 inches (122 cm), plus or minus 2 inches (5 cm), above the walking-working surface.
city-amendments/AM-1.md:40:(b) *Midrails.* Two midrails shall be installed, at one-third and two-thirds of the top rail height, wherever there is no wall or parapet at least 24 inches (61 cm) high. Intermediate vertical members, where used instead of midrails, shall be no more than 12 inches (30 cm) apart.
city-amendments/AM-1.md:42:(c) *Strength.* A guardrail system shall be capable of withstanding, without failure, a force of at least 300 pounds (1,335 N) applied in a downward or outward direction at any point along the top rail. Midrails and intermediate members shall be capable of withstanding a force of at least 200 pounds (890 N).
