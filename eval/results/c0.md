## Recommendation

Ranking by the C.3 criterion (born-digital TextEdit, then reading order, TEDS breaking ties within 0.002): sdk-olmocr (0.001, TEDS 0.747), api-olmocr (0.002, TEDS 0.799), sdk-paddle (0.001, TEDS 0.747), api-glmocr (0.002, TEDS 0.325), api-paddle (0.007, TEDS 0.000), sdk-glmocr (0.013, TEDS 0.677).

- **Primary transcriber: PaddleOCR-VL-1.5 through its official pipeline** (`sdk-paddle`): born-digital TextEdit 0.001, read order 0, table TEDS 0.747 with every table found, scan agreement 0.034. It ties olmOCR-2's pipeline on text, its layout stage emits the element boxes and table HTML the C.3 element schema needs (olmOCR emits markdown only), it is 0.9B and Apache-2.0, and it is the model the spec pre-selected (H.6).
- **Secondary transcriber (independent architecture, divergence checks): olmOCR-2-7B through its official pipeline** (`sdk-olmocr`): born-digital TextEdit 0.001, the best table TextEdit (0.049) and, on the converter's whole-page path, the best TEDS (0.799); it is the one whole-page model that keeps multi-column scans in reading order without a layout stage (api scan agreement 0.029). Qwen2.5-VL lineage against PaddleOCR-VL's ERNIE lineage, Apache-2.0, 7B: about twice the time per page on the 5090.
- **Whole-page API path:** GLM-OCR with `Text Recognition:` is the best single-request transcriber for born-digital pages (TextEdit 0.002 at 5.9 s/doc) but reads three-column scans out of order (scan agreement 0.631) and finds 14 of 20 tables; PaddleOCR-VL given a whole page loops on table cells (13 of 30 documents before the repetition guard, none after it) and still emits no table structure. Element-level models need their layout stage; the converter therefore drives the pipelines for scans and tables and may use the whole-page path only for plain born-digital text.
- **Layout orchestrator:** Marker 2.x in fast mode beats Docling here on every metric (TextEdit 0.003 vs 0.012, TEDS 0.716 vs 0.405) at similar speed; both trail the VLM pipelines on tables. Docling stays the CPU-viable fallback for born-digital layout when no GPU is present; Marker's balanced mode needs a Docker-spawned vLLM for Surya's VLM and was not run.

PaddleOCR-VL's image processor caps the input, so 1288, 1540 and 2048 cost the same 1,253 tokens and score the same: 1288 is enough. The presets in `packages/sect-convert/src/ocr/presets.ts` carry these targets, floor born-digital pages at 100 to 150 DPI, cap at 3 MP, and never upsample a scan past its native DPI (the 1975 and 1985 issues are 100 DPI).
