## Resolve

`sect-harness resolve --run <run_id> --id <section id> --pick ingest|verifier|none|<id>` applies a person's decision to staging, re-runs the evidence checks and consensus for the section, merges it and whatever it unblocked through the merge script, and appends the decision to the examples in `docs/SKILL-ingest.md`; covered by `packages/sect-harness/test/gn2.test.ts`.
