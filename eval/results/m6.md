## Notes

- Re-running `sect index` on unchanged input is a no-op: the fingerprint diff finds nothing and no layer is touched (mode `noop`).
- The n-gram layer of milestone 3b does not exist yet; the incremental pipeline has a slot for it (`layers.ngram` in the manifest).
- Single-file incremental time is dominated by loading the embedding model to embed the changed chunk; the structural layer is rebuilt whole from the parse cache because it is cheap.
