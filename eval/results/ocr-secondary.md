## Reading

GLM 5.3 flash is a reasoning model. With reasoning left on, the hosted reader spent its whole completion budget thinking about the dense 1975 page and returned no content at the cap after four minutes, and truncated the second page; OpenRouter refuses `reasoning.enabled=false` on this endpoint, and the preset now sends `reasoning.effort=minimal`, measured at zero reasoning tokens. With that, the hosted reader is usable where GLM-OCR was not: on page 1, where GLM-OCR looped, it is within 2.5 points of olmOCR-2; on page 2 it is a little behind GLM-OCR; on page 3, a sparse 1985 page with a two-column layout that every reader finds hard, it reads the words in the primary's order but misses or adds more of them than GLM-OCR did. It is the second reader by default from this commit (decision #44); the local GLM-OCR remains available with `SECT_OCR_SECONDARY`.
