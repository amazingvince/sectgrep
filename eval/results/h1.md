## Notes

The live run (OpenRouter z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, concurrency 12) took about 100 minutes of wall time: the 222 sections at two to five a minute, then three fix rounds that re-prompted the sections with validator errors one at a time. The rounds left 31 errors, all of two kinds the agent cannot repair: 17 files some agents had written with staging_write beside the staged sections (flag and note files with hand-made front matter), and references linked to ids or anchors that exist nowhere. The harness now removes stray files before validation and drops references to unknown ids and anchors at section_stage, and sect-harness resubmit re-staged the 270 sections from the run's own records with those rules, no model call, and submitted with 0 errors; the wall time shown in the run table is that resubmit. A first live attempt under the earlier flat staging layout cost 0.95 dollars and was discarded when the container's validator 1 rejected the layout.
