# § 83.18 Rights of legal guardians

For the purposes of [this part](CFR:4-83), the parent of any minor, or the legal guardian of any individual who has been declared to be incompetent due to physical or mental incapacity or age by a court of competent jurisdiction, may act on behalf of the individual.
