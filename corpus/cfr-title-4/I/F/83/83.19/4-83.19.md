# § 83.19 Government contractors

When GAO provides by a contract for the operation by or on behalf of GAO of a system of personnel records to accomplish a function of GAO, GAO shall, consistent with its authority, cause the requirements of [this part](CFR:4-83) to be applied to such system. Any such contractor and any employee of such contractor, if such contract is agreed to on or after the effective date of [this section](CFR:4-83.19), shall be considered, for the purposes of [this part](CFR:4-83), to be an employee of GAO. Contractor employees will be required to observe the confidentiality requirements of [this part](CFR:4-83). Violations of [this part](CFR:4-83) by contractor employees may be a sufficient ground for contract termination.
