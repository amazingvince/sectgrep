# § 6.1 Applicable law and regulations

The provision of [subpart E](CFR:4-28-E), title 5, United States Code and the Office of Personnel Management implementing regulations regarding “Attendance and Leave” apply to Government Accountability Office employees. This includes hours of work, annual leave, sick leave, and other paid leave.
