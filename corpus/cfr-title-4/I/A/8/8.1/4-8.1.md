# § 8.1 Applicable law and regulations

The provisions of [subpart G](CFR:4-28-G), title 5, United States Code and implementing regulations for the Executive Branch covering compensation for work injuries, retirement, unemployment compensation, life insurance, and health insurance apply to Government Accountability Office employees.
