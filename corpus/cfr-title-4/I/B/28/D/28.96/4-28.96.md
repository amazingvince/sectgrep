# § 28.96 Applicability of general procedures

Except where a different procedure is provided for in [this subpart](CFR:4-28-D), the procedures to be followed by all parties in cases arising under this subpart shall be the general procedures as prescribed in [subpart B](CFR:4-28-B) of [this part](CFR:4-28).
