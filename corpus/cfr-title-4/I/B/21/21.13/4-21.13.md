# § 21.13 Nonstatutory protests

(a) GAO will consider protests concerning awards of subcontracts by or for a Federal agency, sales by a Federal agency, or procurements by agencies of the government other than Federal agencies as defined in [§ 21.0(c)](CFR:4-21.0) if the agency involved has agreed in writing to have protests decided by GAO.

(b) The provisions of [this part](CFR:4-21) shall apply to nonstatutory protests except for:

(1) [Section 21.8(d)](CFR:4-21.8#d) and (e) pertaining to recommendations for the payment of costs; and

(2) [Section 21.6](CFR:4-21.6) pertaining to the withholding of award and the suspension of contract performance pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3553(c) and (d).
