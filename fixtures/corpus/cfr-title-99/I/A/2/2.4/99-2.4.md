# § 2.4 Duty to have fall protection

(a) *Unprotected sides and edges.* The employer shall ensure that each employee on a walking-working surface with an unprotected side or edge, as defined in [§ 2.2](CFR:99-2.2#unprotected-sides-and-edges), that is 4 feet (1.2 m) or more above a lower level is protected from falling by one or more of the following: a guardrail system meeting [§ 2.8](CFR:99-2.8); a safety net system; or a personal fall protection system.

(b) *Hoist areas.* The employer shall ensure that each employee in a hoist area is protected from falling 4 feet (1.2 m) or more to a lower level by a guardrail system or a personal fall arrest system. When any portion of a guardrail system is removed to facilitate hoisting and an employee must lean through or over the edge of the access opening, the employee shall be protected by a personal fall arrest system.

(c) *Holes.* The employer shall ensure that each employee is protected from falling through any hole, as defined in [§ 2.2](CFR:99-2.2#hole), that is 4 feet (1.2 m) or more above a lower level by a cover, a guardrail system, or a personal fall protection system, and is protected from tripping into or stepping into a hole by a cover or a guardrail system.

(d) *Dockboards.* The employer shall ensure that each employee on a dockboard is protected from falling 4 feet (1.2 m) or more to a lower level by a guardrail system or a personal fall protection system, except that fall protection is not required when the dockboard is being used solely for materials handling by motorized equipment and employees are protected from falling by other means.

(e) *Runways and similar walkways.* The employer shall ensure that each employee on a runway or similar walkway is protected from falling 4 feet (1.2 m) or more to a lower level by a guardrail system.

(f) *Training.* Each employee who may be exposed to a fall hazard covered by this section shall be trained as required by [§ 2.11](CFR:99-2.11) before being exposed to the hazard.
