# § 2.12 Maintenance, safeguards, and operational features for exit routes

(a) *Unobstructed.* Each exit route shall be kept free of explosive or highly flammable furnishings and decorations, and free of obstructions such as materials, equipment, and locked doors that could impede or block exit travel. An exit route shall not pass through a room that can be locked, such as a bathroom, and shall not lead into a dead end.

(b) *Lighting and marking.* Each exit route shall be adequately lighted so that an employee with normal vision can see along the exit route. Each exit shall be clearly visible and marked by a sign reading "Exit" in plainly legible letters at least 6 inches (15 cm) high. Each doorway or passage along an exit access that could be mistaken for an exit shall be marked "Not an Exit" or with a sign identifying its actual use. Where the direction of travel to the exit is not immediately apparent, signs shall be posted along the exit access indicating the direction of travel.

(c) *Safeguards.* Any fire retardant paints or solutions used in an exit route shall be renewed as often as necessary to maintain their properties. Safeguards designed to protect employees during an emergency, such as sprinkler systems, alarm systems, fire doors, and exit lighting, shall be in proper working order at all times.

(d) *During construction, repair, or alteration.* Employees shall not occupy a workplace until an exit route required by [§ 2.10](CFR:99-2.10) is completed and ready for use, and shall not be exposed to hazards of flammable or explosive substances or equipment used during construction, repair, or alteration that are beyond the normal permissible conditions in the workplace.

(e) *Alarm.* An employee alarm system meeting the requirements of the emergency action plan in [§ 2.13](CFR:99-2.13) shall be installed and maintained in every workplace required to have an emergency action plan.
