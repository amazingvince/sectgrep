# § 2.9 Floor load ratings

(a) *Design loads.* A floor or other walking-working surface used for an occupancy in the following table shall be designed, and shall be maintained, to support at least the uniformly distributed live load shown, in addition to its dead load.

| Occupancy | Minimum design live load |
|---|---|
| Office space | 50 pounds per square foot (2.4 kPa) |
| Corridors and exit access above the first floor | 80 pounds per square foot (3.8 kPa) |
| Light manufacturing | 125 pounds per square foot (6.0 kPa) |
| Heavy manufacturing | 250 pounds per square foot (12.0 kPa) |
| Light storage (warehouse) | 125 pounds per square foot (6.0 kPa) |
| Heavy storage (warehouse) | 250 pounds per square foot (12.0 kPa) |
| Elevated platforms and mezzanines | 125 pounds per square foot (6.0 kPa) |

(b) *Concentrated loads.* In addition to the loads in paragraph (a) of this section, a floor in a manufacturing or storage occupancy shall support a concentrated load of 2,000 pounds (8.9 kN) on any area 2.5 feet (0.76 m) square, placed where it produces the greatest effect.

(c) *Posting.* The employer shall post, in a conspicuous place in each space to which it applies, a plate stating the approved load rating in pounds per square foot. A surface shall not be loaded beyond its posted rating, as required by [§ 2.3(b)](CFR:99-2.3#b).

(d) *Change of occupancy.* A space shall not be used for an occupancy with a higher design load than its rating unless a qualified person, as defined in [§ 1.2](CFR:99-1.2#qualified-person), has determined that the surface will support the new load and the posting under paragraph (c) of this section has been revised.
