# § 2.8 Guardrail systems

(a) *Top rail height.* The top edge height of a top rail, or equivalent guardrail system member, shall be 42 inches (107 cm), plus or minus 3 inches (8 cm), above the walking-working surface. The top edge height may exceed 45 inches (114 cm), provided the guardrail system meets all other criteria of this section.

(b) *Midrails and infill.* Midrails, screens, mesh, intermediate vertical members, solid panels, or equivalent intermediate members shall be installed between the walking-working surface and the top edge of the guardrail system when there is no wall or parapet that is at least 21 inches (53 cm) high. A midrail shall be installed at a height midway between the top edge of the guardrail system and the walking-working surface. Intermediate vertical members shall be no more than 19 inches (48 cm) apart.

(c) *Strength.* A guardrail system shall be capable of withstanding, without failure, a force of at least 200 pounds (890 N) applied in a downward or outward direction within 2 inches (5 cm) of the top edge, at any point along the top rail. Midrails, screens, mesh, and intermediate members shall be capable of withstanding a force of at least 150 pounds (667 N).

(d) *Toeboards.* Where a guardrail system is used at a location where objects could fall to a lower level, a toeboard, as defined in [§ 2.2](CFR:99-2.2#toeboard), at least 3.5 inches (9 cm) high shall be installed at the walking-working surface, with not more than 0.25 inch (0.6 cm) of clearance above the surface.

(e) *Construction.* A guardrail system shall be smooth-surfaced to protect employees from injury and to prevent snagging of clothing. The ends of top rails and midrails shall not overhang the terminal posts, except where the overhang does not constitute a projection hazard. Steel or plastic banding shall not be used as a top rail or midrail.

(f) *Openings.* When a guardrail system is used around a hole, as defined in [§ 2.2](CFR:99-2.2#hole), it shall be installed on all unprotected sides or edges of the hole, and any gate or removable section used at a point of access shall be self-closing or offset so that an employee cannot walk directly into the hole.
