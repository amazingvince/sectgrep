# § 2.14 Fire prevention plans

(a) *Application.* An employer shall have a fire prevention plan whenever a standard in this title requires one. The requirements in this section apply to each such fire prevention plan.

(b) *Written and oral plans.* A fire prevention plan shall be in writing, kept in the workplace, and made available to employees for review. An employer with 10 or fewer employees may communicate the plan orally to employees.

(c) *Minimum elements.* A fire prevention plan shall include:

(1) A list of all major fire hazards, proper handling and storage procedures for hazardous materials, potential ignition sources and their control, and the type of fire protection equipment necessary to control each major hazard;

(2) Procedures to control accumulations of flammable and combustible waste materials, consistent with the housekeeping requirements of [§ 2.3(a)](CFR:99-2.3#a);

(3) Procedures for regular maintenance of safeguards installed on heat-producing equipment to prevent the accidental ignition of combustible materials;

(4) The name or job title of employees responsible for maintaining equipment to prevent or control sources of ignition or fires; and

(5) The name or job title of employees responsible for the control of fuel source hazards.

(d) *Employee information.* An employer shall inform employees upon initial assignment to a job of the fire hazards to which they are exposed, and shall review with each employee those parts of the fire prevention plan necessary for self-protection.

(e) *Combined plans.* The fire prevention plan may be combined with the emergency action plan required by [§ 2.13](CFR:99-2.13), provided the combined plan contains the elements of each.
