# § 2.3 General requirements for walking-working surfaces

(a) *Surface conditions.* The employer shall ensure that all places of employment, passageways, storerooms, service rooms, and walking-working surfaces are kept in a clean, orderly, and sanitary condition; that the floor of each workroom is maintained in a clean and, to the extent feasible, dry condition; and that walking-working surfaces are free of hazards such as sharp or protruding objects, loose boards, corrosion, leaks, spills, snow, and ice.

(b) *Loads.* The employer shall ensure that each walking-working surface can support the maximum intended load for that surface. The design loads for common occupancies are set in [§ 2.9](CFR:99-2.9), and a surface shall not be loaded beyond the rating posted under [§ 2.9(c)](CFR:99-2.9#c).

(c) *Access and egress.* The employer shall provide, and ensure each employee uses, a safe means of access and egress to and from walking-working surfaces. Exit routes are further specified in [§ 2.10](CFR:99-2.10).

(d) *Inspection, maintenance, and repair.* The employer shall ensure that walking-working surfaces are inspected regularly and as necessary, and are maintained in a safe condition. A hazardous condition on a walking-working surface shall be corrected or repaired before an employee uses the surface again; where the correction or repair cannot be made immediately, the hazard shall be guarded to prevent employees from using the surface until it is corrected. Repairs shall be performed or supervised by a qualified person, as defined in [§ 1.2](CFR:99-1.2#qualified-person).
