# § 2.13 Emergency action plans

(a) *Application.* An employer shall have an emergency action plan whenever a standard in this title requires one. The requirements in this section apply to each such emergency action plan.

(b) *Written and oral plans.* An emergency action plan shall be in writing, kept in the workplace, and available to employees for review. An employer with 10 or fewer employees may communicate the plan orally to employees.

(c) *Minimum elements.* An emergency action plan shall include at a minimum:

(1) Procedures for reporting a fire or other emergency;

(2) Procedures for emergency evacuation, including the type of evacuation and the exit route assignments under [§ 2.10](CFR:99-2.10);

(3) Procedures to be followed by employees who remain to operate critical plant operations before they evacuate;

(4) Procedures to account for all employees after evacuation;

(5) Procedures to be followed by employees performing rescue or medical duties; and

(6) The name or job title of every employee who may be contacted by employees who need more information about the plan or an explanation of their duties under the plan.

(d) *Employee alarm system.* An employer shall have and maintain an employee alarm system. The alarm system shall use a distinctive signal for each purpose and shall comply with [§ 2.12(e)](CFR:99-2.12#e).

(e) *Training.* An employer shall designate and train employees to assist in a safe and orderly evacuation of other employees.

(f) *Review.* An employer shall review the emergency action plan with each employee covered by the plan when the plan is developed or the employee is assigned initially to a job, when the employee's responsibilities under the plan change, and when the plan is changed. The fire prevention plan required by [§ 2.14](CFR:99-2.14) may be combined with the emergency action plan.
