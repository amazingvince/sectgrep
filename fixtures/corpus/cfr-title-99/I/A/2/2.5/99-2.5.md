# § 2.5 Ladders; general

(a) *Application.* This section applies to every ladder, as defined in [§ 2.2](CFR:99-2.2#ladder), except a ladder that is an integral part of a machine. Additional requirements for portable ladders are in [§ 2.6](CFR:99-2.6) and for fixed ladders in [§ 2.7](CFR:99-2.7).

(b) *Design and capacity.* The employer shall ensure that each ladder is capable of supporting its maximum intended load without failure, and that rungs, steps, and cleats are parallel, level, and uniformly spaced when the ladder is in position for use, with a spacing of not less than 10 inches (25 cm) and not more than 14 inches (36 cm) measured between the centerlines.

(c) *Surfaces.* Ladder rungs, steps, and cleats shall have a surface that minimizes slipping, such as corrugation, knurling, dimpling, or a skid-resistant coating. A wooden ladder shall not be coated with any material that may obscure structural defects.

(d) *Inspection.* Ladders shall be inspected before initial use in each work shift, and more frequently as necessary, to identify any visible defect that could cause employee injury. A ladder with a structural or other defect shall be immediately tagged "Dangerous: Do Not Use" and removed from service until it is repaired or replaced.

(e) *Use.* Each employee shall face the ladder when climbing up or down, shall use at least one hand to grasp the ladder when climbing, and shall not carry any object or load that could cause the employee to lose balance and fall.
