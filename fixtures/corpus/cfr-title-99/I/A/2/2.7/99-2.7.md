# § 2.7 Fixed ladders

(a) *Application.* This section applies to each fixed ladder, as defined in [§ 2.2](CFR:99-2.2#fixed-ladder), in addition to the requirements of [§ 2.5](CFR:99-2.5).

(b) *Fall protection.* The employer shall ensure that each fixed ladder that extends more than 24 feet (7.3 m) above a lower level is equipped with a personal fall arrest system or a ladder safety system. A cage or well is not a means of fall protection under this paragraph, except that:

(1) A cage or well installed before January 1, 2026 may continue in use until December 31, 2036, provided it is maintained in accordance with paragraph (e) of this section; and

(2) When a fixed ladder, cage, or well is replaced, or a new fixed ladder is installed, on or after January 1, 2026, the ladder shall be equipped with a personal fall arrest system or a ladder safety system, and a cage or well shall not be installed as a substitute.

(c) *Landing platforms.* Where a ladder safety system or personal fall arrest system is used, the ladder shall have a rest platform at intervals of not more than 150 feet (45.7 m). Where a cage or well remains in use under paragraph (b)(1) of this section, the ladder shall have landing platforms at intervals of not more than 50 feet (15.2 m), with the ladder sections offset so that a fall is arrested at each platform.

(d) *Top of ladder.* The side rails of a through or side-step fixed ladder shall extend at least 42 inches (1.1 m) above the top of the access level or landing platform, and the landing shall be protected by a guardrail system meeting [§ 2.8](CFR:99-2.8) except at the ladder opening.

(e) *Maintenance.* Fixed ladders and their fall protection systems shall be inspected annually and maintained free of corrosion, deformation, and loose or missing rungs and fasteners.
