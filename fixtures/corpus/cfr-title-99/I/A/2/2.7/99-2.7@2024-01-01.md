# § 2.7 Fixed ladders

(a) *Application.* This section applies to each fixed ladder, as defined in [§ 2.2](CFR:99-2.2#fixed-ladder), in addition to the requirements of [§ 2.5](CFR:99-2.5).

(b) *Fall protection.* The employer shall ensure that each fixed ladder that extends more than 24 feet (7.3 m) above a lower level is equipped with a personal fall arrest system, a ladder safety system, a cage, or a well. A cage or well installed on or before the effective date of this section is an acceptable means of fall protection for the life of the ladder, provided it is maintained in accordance with paragraph (e) of this section.

(c) *Landing platforms.* Where a ladder safety system or personal fall arrest system is used, the ladder shall have a rest platform at intervals of not more than 150 feet (45.7 m). Where a cage or well is used, the ladder shall have landing platforms at intervals of not more than 50 feet (15.2 m), with the ladder sections offset so that a fall is arrested at each platform.

(d) *Top of ladder.* The side rails of a through or side-step fixed ladder shall extend at least 42 inches (1.1 m) above the top of the access level or landing platform, and the landing shall be protected by a guardrail system meeting [§ 2.8](CFR:99-2.8) except at the ladder opening.

(e) *Maintenance.* Fixed ladders and their fall protection systems shall be inspected annually and maintained free of corrosion, deformation, and loose or missing rungs and fasteners.
