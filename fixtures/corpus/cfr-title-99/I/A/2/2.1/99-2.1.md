# § 2.1 Scope and application

(a) This part applies to all general industry workplaces covered by [§ 1.3](CFR:99-1.3). It covers all walking-working surfaces, including floors, stairs, ladders, ramps, scaffolds, elevated walkways, dockboards, and roofs, and all exit routes, unless a provision of this part states otherwise.

(b) This part does not apply to:

(1) An operation exempted by [§ 1.5](CFR:99-1.5);

(2) A powered platform or a vehicle-mounted work platform, which is covered by its own standard; or

(3) A ladder used in emergency operations such as firefighting or rescue, or a ladder that is an integral part of a machine or equipment.

(c) Terms used in this part are defined in [§ 2.2](CFR:99-2.2). A term not defined there has the meaning given in [§ 1.2](CFR:99-1.2).
