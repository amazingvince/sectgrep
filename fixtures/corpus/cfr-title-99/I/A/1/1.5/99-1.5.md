# § 1.5 Exemptions

(a) This title does not apply to:

(1) Domestic household employment;

(2) An agricultural operation that employed no more than ten employees at any time during the preceding twelve months and that has not maintained a temporary labor camp during that period; or

(3) A mining operation, to the extent the operation is regulated under the Fixture Mine Safety Act.

(b) An employer exempt under paragraph (a) of this section remains subject to [§ 3.5](CFR:99-3.5) (reporting of fatalities and severe injuries).

(c) The partial exemption from routine recordkeeping for small employers in [§ 3.8](CFR:99-3.8) is independent of this section. An employer may be covered by this title and still be exempt from routine recordkeeping.
