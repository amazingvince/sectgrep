# § 1.2 Definitions

As used in this title:

*Competent person* means a person who is capable of identifying existing and predictable hazards in the surroundings or working conditions and who has authorization to take prompt corrective measures to eliminate them.

*Employee* means an individual employed by an employer, including a part-time, temporary, or seasonal worker, but not an independent contractor.

*Employer* means a person engaged in a business affecting commerce who has employees, but does not include a State or political subdivision of a State except as provided in [§ 1.3(b)](CFR:99-1.3#b).

*Establishment* means a single physical location where business is conducted or where services or industrial operations are performed. For the purposes of [Part 3](CFR:99-3) of this title, an employer with more than one physical location keeps separate records for each establishment that is expected to operate for one year or longer.

*Hazardous condition* means a condition, practice, or equipment failure that could reasonably be expected to cause death or serious physical harm.

*Qualified person* means a person who, by possession of a recognized degree, certificate, or professional standing, or by extensive knowledge, training, and experience, has demonstrated the ability to solve or resolve problems relating to the subject matter, the work, or the project.

*Workday* means any day on which an employee performs work at the direction of the employer, whether or not the employee is present at the establishment.
