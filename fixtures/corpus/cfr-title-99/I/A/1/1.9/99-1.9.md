# § 1.9 Inspections and citations

(a) *Authority to inspect.* An authorized representative of the Administration may, upon presenting credentials, enter without delay and at reasonable times any establishment where work is performed by an employee of an employer covered by this title, and may inspect all pertinent conditions, structures, machines, devices, equipment, materials, and records. Records kept under [Part 3](CFR:99-3) of this title shall be provided as described in [§ 3.6(c)](CFR:99-3.6#c).

(b) *Walkaround.* A representative of the employer and a representative authorized by the employees shall be given an opportunity to accompany the inspector during the physical inspection of the workplace.

(c) *Citations.* If an inspection reveals a violation of this title, the Administration shall issue a citation to the employer with reasonable promptness, and in any event within six months after the violation occurred. The citation shall describe the violation with particularity, cite the provision violated, and fix a reasonable time for abatement.

(d) *Classification.* Each violation is classified as *other-than-serious*, *serious*, *willful*, or *repeated*. A *serious* violation is one in which there is a substantial probability that death or serious physical harm could result and the employer knew, or with the exercise of reasonable diligence could have known, of the violation. Penalty amounts by classification are set in [§ 1.10](CFR:99-1.10).

(e) *Contest.* An employer may contest a citation or a proposed penalty by filing a notice of contest within 15 working days after receiving the citation.
