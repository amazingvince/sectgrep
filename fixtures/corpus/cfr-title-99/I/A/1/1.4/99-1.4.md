# § 1.4 Employer duties

(a) *General duty.* Each employer shall furnish to each of its employees a place of employment that is free from hazardous conditions, as defined in [§ 1.2](CFR:99-1.2#hazardous-condition), and shall comply with the standards in this title that apply to its workplaces.

(b) *Training.* Each employer shall train each employee in the hazards of the employee's work and in the requirements of this title that apply to that work. Training shall be provided before initial assignment, periodically thereafter, and whenever a change in the workplace or in the assigned work introduces a hazard on which the employee has not been trained. Training on fall hazards is further specified in [§ 2.11](CFR:99-2.11).

(c) *Hazard correction.* Each employer shall correct a hazardous condition promptly upon discovering it. Where immediate correction is not feasible, the employer shall protect employees from the hazard by other means until it is corrected, and shall record the condition and the interim measures as provided in [§ 3.2](CFR:99-3.2).

(d) *Posting.* Each employer shall post the notices required by [§ 1.6](CFR:99-1.6).

(e) *Records.* Each employer shall keep the records required by [Part 3](CFR:99-3) of this title and shall make them available as provided in [§ 3.6](CFR:99-3.6).
