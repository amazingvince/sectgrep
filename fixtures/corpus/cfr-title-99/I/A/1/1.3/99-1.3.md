# § 1.3 Applicability

(a) This title applies to every employer, as defined in [§ 1.2](CFR:99-1.2#employer), and to every workplace at which that employer's employees perform work, except as provided in [§ 1.5](CFR:99-1.5).

(b) A State or political subdivision of a State is an employer under this title only with respect to the employees of a public school, public hospital, or public transit operation that it operates.

(c) Where a workplace is subject to a standard of another Federal agency that addresses the same hazard, this title does not apply to that hazard for as long as the other standard is in effect. An employer relying on this paragraph shall identify the other standard in the records kept under [§ 3.4](CFR:99-3.4).

(d) A workplace on the premises of a Federal facility is covered by this title when the employer is a private contractor performing work there.
