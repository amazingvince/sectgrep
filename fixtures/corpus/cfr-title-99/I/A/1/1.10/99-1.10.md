# § 1.10 Civil penalties

(a) The civil penalty for a violation classified under [§ 1.9(d)](CFR:99-1.9#d) shall not exceed the maximum, and for a willful or repeated violation shall not be less than the minimum, in the following table.

| Violation class | Minimum penalty | Maximum penalty |
|---|---|---|
| Other-than-serious | $0 | $16,131 |
| Serious | $1,190 | $16,131 |
| Willful | $11,524 | $161,323 |
| Repeated | $0 | $161,323 |
| Failure to abate | $0 | $16,131 per day |

(b) *Reductions.* The proposed penalty for an other-than-serious or serious violation is reduced by up to 70 percent for an employer with 25 or fewer employees, by up to 25 percent for an employer with a written safety program and a history of good faith, and by 20 percent for an employer with no serious, willful, or repeated citation in the preceding five years. Reductions do not apply to willful or repeated violations.

(c) *Failure to abate.* An employer that fails to correct a cited violation within the abatement period fixed under [§ 1.9(c)](CFR:99-1.9#c) may be assessed the daily penalty in paragraph (a) of this section for each day the violation continues.

(d) *Adjustment.* The amounts in paragraph (a) of this section are adjusted for inflation each January 15 and published in the Fixture Register.
