# § 1.7 Variances

(a) *Temporary variance.* An employer may apply for a temporary variance from a standard in [Part 2](CFR:99-2) of this title if the employer:

(1) Is unable to comply with the standard by its effective date because of the unavailability of professional or technical personnel, materials, or equipment, or because necessary construction or alteration of facilities cannot be completed by that date;

(2) Is taking all available steps to safeguard its employees against the hazards covered by the standard; and

(3) Has an effective program for coming into compliance with the standard as quickly as practicable.

(b) *Permanent variance.* An employer may apply for a permanent variance from a standard in [Part 2](CFR:99-2) of this title if it demonstrates by a preponderance of the evidence that the conditions, practices, means, methods, operations, or processes it uses or proposes to use will provide employment and places of employment that are as safe and healthful as those that would prevail if it complied with the standard.

(c) *Duration.* A temporary variance may be granted for the period needed to achieve compliance or one year, whichever is shorter, and may be renewed no more than twice. A permanent variance remains in effect until it is modified or revoked under [§ 1.8(e)](CFR:99-1.8#e).

(d) *Effect.* A variance does not relieve an employer of the recordkeeping and reporting duties of [Part 3](CFR:99-3) of this title.

(e) An application for a variance is filed and decided in accordance with [§ 1.8](CFR:99-1.8).
