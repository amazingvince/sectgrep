# § 1.1 Purpose and scope

(a) This title establishes occupational safety standards for the workplaces of employers covered by [§ 1.3](CFR:99-1.3). The standards in [Part 2](CFR:99-2) of this title address walking-working surfaces and means of egress. [Part 3](CFR:99-3) of this title addresses the recording and reporting of occupational injuries and illnesses.

(b) Terms used throughout this title are defined in [§ 1.2](CFR:99-1.2). A definition that applies to only one part appears in that part.

(c) The exemptions in [§ 1.5](CFR:99-1.5) apply to every part of this title unless the part states otherwise.

(d) A local jurisdiction may adopt an amendment that is more protective of employees than a provision of this title. Where a local amendment overrides a section of this title, or narrows a paragraph of a section, the local amendment governs within that jurisdiction and this title governs elsewhere.
