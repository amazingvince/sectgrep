# § 1.6 Posting of notices

(a) Each employer shall post, at each establishment, in a conspicuous place where notices to employees are customarily posted, the official notice informing employees of their protections and obligations under this title.

(b) Each employer required to keep the summary described in [§ 3.3(b)](CFR:99-3.3#b) shall post the summary for the preceding calendar year from February 1 to April 30 of the year following the year covered by the summary.

(c) An employer shall replace a posted notice that has become damaged, illegible, or obscured.

(d) A notice posted under this section is not a record for purposes of the retention period in [§ 3.4](CFR:99-3.4), but the summary posted under paragraph (b) of this section is retained as provided there after the posting period ends.

(e) Where employees at an establishment do not read English, the employer shall also post the notice in the language or languages spoken by those employees, if the Administration has published the notice in that language.
