# § 1.8 Petitions for variance; procedure

(a) *Contents.* A petition for a variance under [§ 1.7](CFR:99-1.7) shall contain:

(1) The name and address of the employer and of each establishment covered by the petition;

(2) A citation to the standard, or the portion of the standard, from which the variance is sought;

(3) A statement of the conditions, practices, means, methods, operations, or processes the employer uses or proposes to use, and the facts showing that they provide employment that is as safe and healthful as compliance with the standard; and

(4) A certification that the employer has informed its employees of the petition as required by paragraph (b) of this section.

(b) *Employee notice.* The employer shall give a copy of the petition to the employees' authorized representative, post a summary of the petition where the notices required by [§ 1.6](CFR:99-1.6) are posted, and inform employees that they may request a hearing.

(c) *Deadlines.* A petition shall be filed, and will be decided, within the periods in the following table.

| Variance type | File no later than | Decision due within |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary | 30 days before the standard's effective date | 60 days of filing |
| Temporary renewal | 90 days before the variance expires | 45 days of filing |
| Permanent | Any time | 120 days of filing |

(d) *Interim order.* The Administration may issue an interim order allowing the employer to continue its current practices while the petition is pending, if the petition shows that employees will be protected in the interim.

(e) *Modification or revocation.* A variance may be modified or revoked on the petition of the employer or of an affected employee, or on the Administration's own motion, after notice and an opportunity for a hearing.
