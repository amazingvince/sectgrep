# § 3.8 Partial exemption for small employers

(a) *Basic requirement.* If the employer had ten or fewer employees at all times during the last calendar year, the employer does not need to keep the injury and illness records required by [§ 3.3](CFR:99-3.3), unless the Administration informs the employer in writing that it must keep records under paragraph (c) of this section.

(b) *Counting employees.* The employer counts all employees of the company as a whole, not per establishment, including part-time, seasonal, and temporary employees, at the peak employment of the year. A company that at any time in the last calendar year had more than ten employees is not exempt under this section.

(c) *Survey.* An employer that is exempt under paragraph (a) of this section shall keep records if it is notified in writing that it has been selected to participate in an annual survey of occupational injuries and illnesses, and shall report the survey data as directed in the notice.

(d) *Reporting continues.* This section does not relieve any employer of the duty to report fatalities and severe injuries under [§ 3.5](CFR:99-3.5), and it is independent of the coverage exemptions in [§ 1.5](CFR:99-1.5).
