# § 3.4 Retention and updating

(a) *Retention.* The employer shall save the log, the annual summary, and the incident reports required by [§ 3.3](CFR:99-3.3) for five years following the end of the calendar year that the records cover. The training records required by [§ 2.11(e)](CFR:99-2.11#e) and the documentation required by [§ 1.3(c)](CFR:99-1.3#c) shall be retained for the same period.

(b) *Updating the log.* During the retention period, the employer shall update the log to include newly discovered recordable injuries or illnesses and to show any changes that have occurred in the classification of previously recorded cases. If the description or outcome of a case changes, the employer shall remove or line out the original entry and enter the new information.

(c) *Summary and incident reports.* The employer is not required to update the annual summary or the incident reports, but may do so.

(d) *Change in business ownership.* If the business changes ownership, the employer is responsible for recording and reporting work-related injuries and illnesses only for that period of the year during which it owned the establishment, and shall transfer the records required by this part to the new owner, who shall save them as provided in paragraph (a) of this section.
