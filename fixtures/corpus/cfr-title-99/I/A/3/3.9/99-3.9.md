# § 3.9 Definitions

The following definitions apply to this part:

*Days away from work* means calendar days, including weekends, holidays, and vacation days, on which the employee would not have been able to work because of the injury or illness, counted from the day after the injury or illness, up to a cap of 180 days.

*First aid* means only the following treatments, regardless of who provides them: using a non-prescription medication at non-prescription strength; administering a tetanus immunization; cleaning, flushing, or soaking a wound on the surface of the skin; using a wound covering such as a bandage, gauze pad, or butterfly bandage; using hot or cold therapy; using a non-rigid means of support such as an elastic bandage or wrap; using a temporary immobilization device while transporting an accident victim; drilling a fingernail or toenail to relieve pressure, or draining fluid from a blister; using an eye patch; removing a foreign body from the eye using only irrigation or a cotton swab; removing a splinter or foreign material from an area other than the eye by irrigation, tweezers, cotton swab, or other simple means; using a finger guard; using massage; and drinking fluids for relief of heat stress.

*Medical treatment* means the management and care of a patient to combat disease or disorder. Medical treatment does not include a visit to a physician or other licensed health care professional solely for observation or counseling, a diagnostic procedure such as an x-ray or blood test, or first aid as defined in this section.

*Physician or other licensed health care professional* means an individual whose legally permitted scope of practice allows the individual to independently perform, or be delegated the responsibility to perform, the activities described by this part.

*Restricted work* means that, as the result of a work-related injury or illness, the employer keeps the employee from performing one or more of the routine functions of the job or from working the full workday the employee would otherwise have been scheduled to work, or a physician or other licensed health care professional recommends either of those restrictions.

*Work environment* means the establishment and other locations where one or more employees are working or are present as a condition of their employment. The work environment includes not only physical locations but also the equipment and materials used by the employee during the course of work.
