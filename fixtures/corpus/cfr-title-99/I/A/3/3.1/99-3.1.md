# § 3.1 Purpose

(a) This part requires employers to record and report work-related fatalities, injuries, and illnesses. Keeping the records required by this part is one of the employer duties listed in [§ 1.4(e)](CFR:99-1.4#e), and the records inform the hazard correction required by [§ 1.4(c)](CFR:99-1.4#c).

(b) Recording or reporting a work-related injury, illness, or fatality does not mean that the employer or the employee was at fault, that a rule of this title has been violated, or that the employee is eligible for workers' compensation or other benefits.

(c) The criteria for deciding whether a case is recordable are in [§ 3.2](CFR:99-3.2). An employer with ten or fewer employees may be partially exempt from this part under [§ 3.8](CFR:99-3.8), but no employer is exempt from the reporting duty in [§ 3.5](CFR:99-3.5).
