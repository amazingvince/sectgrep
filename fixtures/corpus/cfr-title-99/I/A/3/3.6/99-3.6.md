# § 3.6 Employee access to records

(a) *Employee involvement.* The employer shall inform each employee how to report a work-related injury or illness and shall set up a way for employees to report promptly. The employer shall not discharge or in any manner discriminate against an employee for reporting a work-related injury or illness.

(b) *Access to the log and summary.* When an employee, former employee, personal representative, or authorized employee representative asks for copies of the log or the annual summary for an establishment where the employee works or worked, the employer shall provide the copies by the end of the next business day, free of charge for the first request in a calendar year.

(c) *Access by the Administration.* When an authorized representative of the Administration asks for records during an inspection under [§ 1.9](CFR:99-1.9), the employer shall provide copies of the log, the annual summary, and the incident reports within four business hours.

(d) *Access to incident reports.* When an employee, former employee, or personal representative asks for a copy of the incident report describing an injury or illness to that employee, the employer shall provide it by the end of the next business day. When an authorized employee representative asks for copies of the incident reports for an establishment, the employer shall provide them within seven calendar days, with the personal information removed except for the description of the injury or illness.

(e) *Privacy cases.* Names shall be withheld from the copies of the log provided under this section for cases identified as privacy concern cases under [§ 3.7](CFR:99-3.7).
