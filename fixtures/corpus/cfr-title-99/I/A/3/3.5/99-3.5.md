# § 3.5 Reporting fatalities and severe injuries

(a) *Basic requirement.* Every employer covered by this title shall report to the Administration each work-related event in the following table within the period shown, measured from the time the employer learns of the event. This section applies to every employer, including an employer exempt from routine recordkeeping under [§ 1.5](CFR:99-1.5) or [§ 3.8](CFR:99-3.8).

| Event | Report within | Reportable if the event occurs |
|---|---|---|
| Fatality | 8 hours | Within 30 days of the work-related incident |
| In-patient hospitalization of one or more employees | 24 hours | Within 24 hours of the work-related incident |
| Amputation | 24 hours | Within 24 hours of the work-related incident |
| Loss of an eye | 24 hours | Within 24 hours of the work-related incident |

(b) *How to report.* A report shall be made by telephone to the nearest area office, by the toll-free telephone number, or through the electronic reporting application, and shall give the establishment name, the location and time of the incident, the type of reportable event, the number of employees affected, the names of the employees, a contact person and telephone number, and a brief description of the incident.

(c) *Motor vehicle accidents.* An employer is not required to report a fatality or in-patient hospitalization that results from a motor vehicle accident on a public street or highway, except in a construction work zone, but shall record the case on the log if it is recordable under [§ 3.2](CFR:99-3.2).

(d) *Heart attacks.* An employer is not required to report a fatality or in-patient hospitalization that results from a heart attack, unless the heart attack was caused by an event in the work environment. The employer shall record the case if it is recordable under [§ 3.2](CFR:99-3.2).

(e) *Diagnostic hospitalization.* An in-patient hospitalization for observation or diagnostic testing only is not a reportable event under this section.
