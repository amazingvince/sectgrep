# § 3.3 Recording forms

(a) *Log.* The employer shall keep, for each establishment as defined in [§ 1.2](CFR:99-1.2#establishment), a log (Form 300) of every recordable injury and illness, entering each case within seven calendar days of receiving information that a recordable injury or illness has occurred. Each entry shall identify the employee, the date, the location, and a description of the injury or illness, and shall classify the case by outcome.

(b) *Annual summary.* At the end of each calendar year the employer shall review the log for completeness and accuracy, create an annual summary (Form 300A) of the injuries and illnesses recorded on the log, certify the summary, and post it as required by [§ 1.6(b)](CFR:99-1.6#b). The summary shall be certified by a company executive.

(c) *Incident report.* The employer shall complete an injury and illness incident report (Form 301), or an equivalent form, for each recordable injury or illness entered on the log, within seven calendar days of receiving information that the case has occurred.

(d) *Equivalent forms.* The employer may use an equivalent form in place of any form named in this section if it contains the same information, is as readable and understandable, and is completed using the same instructions.

(e) *Multiple establishments.* An employer with more than one establishment shall keep separate forms for each establishment expected to be in operation for one year or longer, and may keep one set of forms for all of its short-term establishments.
