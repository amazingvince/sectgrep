# Fixture Safety Administration

## 99 CFR Part 2

### Walking-Working Surfaces; Fixed Ladders; Phase-Out of Cages and Wells

**AGENCY:** Fixture Safety Administration.

**ACTION:** Final rule.

**SUMMARY:** The Fixture Safety Administration is revising the fall protection requirement for fixed ladders in Title 99. The rule removes cages and wells from the list of accepted means of fall protection on fixed ladders that extend more than 24 feet above a lower level, requires a personal fall arrest system or a ladder safety system on new and replaced ladders, and allows existing cages and wells to remain in use for a phase-out period ending December 31, 2036.

**DATES:** This rule is effective January 1, 2026.

## Supplementary information

Under [§ 2.7(b)](CFR:99-2.7#b) of Title 99 as issued in 2024, a cage or well was an acceptable means of fall protection on a fixed ladder for the life of the ladder. Incident data collected under [Part 3](CFR:99-3) since that date show that cages and wells do not arrest a fall; they contain the falling employee within the ladder envelope and can themselves cause injury. Ladder safety systems and personal fall arrest systems, by contrast, arrest a fall within a short distance.

The Administration considered and rejected an immediate removal requirement. Replacing every existing cage would impose a large cost with little benefit where the ladder is rarely climbed. A ten-year phase-out lets employers replace cages and wells as part of normal maintenance, while ensuring that no new cage or well is installed as a substitute for a fall arrest system.

The rule does not change the general ladder requirements of [§ 2.5](CFR:99-2.5), the landing platform spacing where a cage remains in use, or the guardrail requirement of [§ 2.8](CFR:99-2.8) at the top of a ladder.

## Amendatory instructions

Accordingly, Part 2 of Title 99 is amended as follows:

1. The authority citation for Part 2 continues to read as follows: Authority: 99 U.S.C. 651, 655, 657.

2. [§ 2.7](CFR:99-2.7) is amended by revising paragraph (b) to read as follows:

> **§ 2.7 Fixed ladders**
>
> \* \* \* \* \*
>
> (b) *Fall protection.* The employer shall ensure that each fixed ladder that extends more than 24 feet (7.3 m) above a lower level is equipped with a personal fall arrest system or a ladder safety system. A cage or well is not a means of fall protection under this paragraph, except that:
>
> (1) A cage or well installed before January 1, 2026 may continue in use until December 31, 2036, provided it is maintained in accordance with paragraph (e) of this section; and
>
> (2) When a fixed ladder, cage, or well is replaced, or a new fixed ladder is installed, on or after January 1, 2026, the ladder shall be equipped with a personal fall arrest system or a ladder safety system, and a cage or well shall not be installed as a substitute.
>
> \* \* \* \* \*
