# AM-2 Training frequency and exit access width (local amendments to § 1.4(b) and § 2.10(b))

(a) *Training frequency.* Within the City of Fixture, the periodic training required by [§ 1.4(b)](CFR:99-1.4#b) of Title 99 shall be provided at least once every 12 months, and the phrase "periodically thereafter" in that paragraph is read as "at least annually thereafter". The remainder of § 1.4 applies unchanged.

(b) *Exit access width.* Within the City of Fixture, the minimum width of an exit access under [§ 2.10(b)](CFR:99-2.10#b) of Title 99 is 36 inches (91 cm) rather than 28 inches (71 cm). The ceiling height requirement and the remainder of § 2.10 apply unchanged.

(c) *Applicability.* Paragraph (b) of this amendment applies to exit routes in a building constructed, or substantially altered, on or after March 1, 2025. Paragraph (a) applies to every employer in the city from that date.
