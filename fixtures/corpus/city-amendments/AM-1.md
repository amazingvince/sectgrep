# AM-1 Guardrail systems (local amendment to § 2.8)

Within the City of Fixture, [§ 2.8](CFR:99-2.8) of Title 99 is replaced by the following:

(a) *Top rail height.* The top edge height of a top rail, or equivalent guardrail system member, shall be 48 inches (122 cm), plus or minus 2 inches (5 cm), above the walking-working surface.

(b) *Midrails.* Two midrails shall be installed, at one-third and two-thirds of the top rail height, wherever there is no wall or parapet at least 24 inches (61 cm) high. Intermediate vertical members, where used instead of midrails, shall be no more than 12 inches (30 cm) apart.

(c) *Strength.* A guardrail system shall be capable of withstanding, without failure, a force of at least 300 pounds (1,335 N) applied in a downward or outward direction at any point along the top rail. Midrails and intermediate members shall be capable of withstanding a force of at least 200 pounds (890 N).

(d) *Toeboards.* A toeboard at least 4 inches (10 cm) high shall be installed wherever a guardrail system is required, whether or not objects could fall to a lower level.

(e) *Other criteria.* Paragraphs (e) and (f) of § 2.8 of Title 99 (construction, and openings around holes) apply within the city unchanged.

(f) *Applicability.* This amendment applies to a guardrail system installed or substantially altered on or after March 1, 2025. A guardrail system that complied with § 2.8 of Title 99 before that date may remain in service until it is replaced.
