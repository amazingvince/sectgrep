# Exit access width across jurisdictions

Synthesized note. Cite the sections, not this page.

| Jurisdiction | Minimum exit access width | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Base title (everywhere outside the City of Fixture) | 28 inches (71 cm) | [§ 2.10(b)](CFR:99-2.10#b) |
| City of Fixture, buildings constructed or substantially altered on or after 2025-03-01 | 36 inches (91 cm) | [AM-2(b)](CITY:AM-2#b) |
| City of Fixture, older buildings | 28 inches (71 cm) | [AM-2(c)](CITY:AM-2#c) applies the wider minimum only to newer construction |

What the amendment leaves alone:

- The 7 feet 6 inch ceiling height in [§ 2.10(b)](CFR:99-2.10#b).
- The number of exit routes in [§ 2.10(a)](CFR:99-2.10#a) and the door and capacity rules in [§ 2.10(e)](CFR:99-2.10#e) and [§ 2.10(f)](CFR:99-2.10#f).
- Day-to-day maintenance, lighting, and marking in [§ 2.12](CFR:99-2.12).

Related: the same amendment also narrows the training frequency in [§ 1.4(b)](CFR:99-1.4#b) to at least annually.
